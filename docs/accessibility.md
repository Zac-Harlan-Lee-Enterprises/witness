# Accessibility plan and status

**Target:** [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) Level AA for every screen and control, plus game-specific accessibility (motor, cognitive, reading and hearing needs) that WCAG does not cover.

**Status (2026-09-24):** every feature below is implemented and checked in the code. The automated checks pass: axe-core in jsdom, axe-core in a real Chromium browser with the WCAG 2.2 AA rule tags including colour contrast, a keyboard-only E2E run and touch E2E runs. **This is not a conformance claim.** No formal audit has been done, and no disabled players or real screen-reader users have tested the game yet. See [Known gaps](#known-gaps-and-risks).

---

## 1. Design principles

1. **The world canvas is decorative.** The Phaser canvas is `aria-hidden` and not focusable. Everything a player needs is also shown as real HTML: where you are, what to do next, who is nearby, every conversation, every puzzle and every menu.
2. **You never have to steer.** The **"Go to…"** list names every person, object and exit in the current place. Choosing one walks you there, or jumps you there with Instant travel, and then talks, examines or leaves. The whole chapter can be finished this way, and the E2E tests finish it this way.
3. **Nothing is timed.** Conversations wait for you. Puzzles have no clock and no penalty. The in-story "time of day" moves only when you make decisions or travel, never in real time.
4. **Sound is never needed.** Every sound goes with something written on screen.
5. **Settings belong to the device and apply at once.** Accessibility needs have to work before a profile is picked, so settings are stored per device, shared by all profiles, and take effect as soon as you change them ([`src/domain/settings.ts`](../src/domain/settings.ts)).

**A real issue found and fixed.** The **locked chapter cards** on the chapter-select screen had been faded with `opacity`, which failed colour contrast (WCAG 1.4.3). Only the browser-level check can catch this kind of problem, because colour contrast is switched off in jsdom. That is why both layers exist.

## 4. Why sound effects need no captions

The game has no voice acting. Its sound effects are synthesised tones ([`synth-audio.ts`](../src/infrastructure/audio/synth-audio.ts)), and **each one plays at the same moment as something already shown in text**:

| Sound effect | Played when | The same information in text |
|---|---|---|
| `interact` | You talk to or examine something | The conversation opens, or a toast labelled "Note:" explains why not |
| `door` | You change place | The HUD place name changes, and the live region announces "Place. Description." |
| `item` | You receive an item | A "Received: …" toast (also in the Satchel) |
| `journal` | You find a clue | A "New clue: …" toast (also in the Journal) |
| `quest` | A quest starts or ends | A "New quest" / "Side quest" / "Quest resolved" toast (also in the Quest log) |
| `solved` | A puzzle is solved | "✓ Solved!" in a `role="status"` region, with an explanation |
| `error`, `page` | *Defined in `SfxId` but not played anywhere today* | — |

Captions are therefore used only for **ambience and music** (row 17), which set the mood but carry no information.

Content authors can also request a sound through the `playSound` effect ([`src/domain/effects.ts`](../src/domain/effects.ts)). Chapter 1 never uses it. Any future use must come with visible text, and a content-validation rule for that would be a sensible addition.

## The teaser film (before Chapter 1)

The film before Chapter 1 ([`TeaserPlayer.tsx`](../src/features/teaser/TeaserPlayer.tsx)) never stands between a player and the game:

- **Skip** is always visible and has focus from the start; Escape skips from anywhere, and Enter or Space too when no other button has focus. **Pause/Play** stops and starts the film and its music (2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide).
- The film has no words or sound of its own. Its words are **real text** over it, cued to its time, set in the game's type at the player's text size, white on a darkened band with a shadow, and in a polite live region, so screen readers announce each card once. The film's content is described in words for screen readers.
- The film is **muted**; its music is the game's own synthesiser, so it follows the music volume and mute settings, and it stops on pause, skip or end.
- **Reduced motion** (the setting, or the device's preference): the film does not play. The poster is shown with the words, stepped through with **Next**; Skip is still there.
- **When the film can't load** (offline, a missing or unsupported file, nothing after 8 s), the same stills and words are shown: the chapter is never blocked.
- It fits any screen (letterboxed), with 44-pixel controls clear of the safe areas.

Tested in [`tests/ui/teaser.test.tsx`](../tests/ui/teaser.test.tsx) (axe, focus, keys, cues, reduced motion, fallbacks) and [`e2e/teaser.spec.ts`](../e2e/teaser.spec.ts) (axe in the browser, a phone screen, a film that can't load).
