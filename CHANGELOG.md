# Changelog

Witness follows [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/). Before 1.0, a minor version adds player-facing features and a patch fixes things. 1.0 comes when the owner has approved all the chapters' content. The version shows on the title screen (`package.json`).

## 0.8.0 — 2026-09-30
- Longer chapters: new errands, side quests, people, puzzles and conversations in all four chapters (20–30+ minutes each for a player who explores) (#18, #19).
- New story text stays in review until the owner approves it.

## 0.7.0 — 2026-09-30
- Six new puzzle types, so each later chapter has its own: trim and netting (Galilee), logic grid and floor plan (Bethlehem), map and dyeing (Colossae) (#16).

## 0.6.1 — 2026-09-30
- New versions install themselves outside a chapter (#14).
- The chapter ending and title screen no longer name only Chapter 1 (#15).

## 0.6.0 — 2026-09-28
- A 57-second animated teaser film before Chapter 1, replayable from chapter select (#12).
- Pick a reply with the arrow keys and Space (#13).

## 0.5.1 — 2026-09-27
- Hawk shadows fly head first; the measuring puzzle says where the water goes (#11).

## 0.5.0 — 2026-09-27
- Portraits show glad, worried, sad, angry, surprised and afraid faces to match each line (#10).

## 0.4.1 — 2026-09-27
- Hovering a dialogue choice no longer brings up a scrollbar (#9).

## 0.4.0 — 2026-09-27
- New versions reach players already in a game: Save and update in the pause menu (#7).
- Portraits rebuilt on an anatomical head model (#8).

## 0.3.1 — 2026-09-26
- Phone texture memory, art polish, and people's shadows indoors (#6).

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-26
- The World English Bible text is shown (#5).

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-26
- Chapters 2–4, pre-rendered 3D art for every place, realistic portraits, and an engine graphics upgrade (#4).

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-26
- The first vertical slice: Chapter 1, The Road to Jericho, with the pre-rendered market (#1).
